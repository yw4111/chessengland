About Chess England

Chess England runs rated chess tournaments across London and beyond, organised by Adam Raoof — a long-standing fixture of the London chess scene. From monthly blitz to weekend congresses, the aim is simple: well-run events where players of every level get a proper game.

Every tournament is announced, entered and reported through The Chess Circuit — the newsletter that keeps hundreds of players up to date on where to play next.

“The game of chess is my passion. If you want to play chess tournaments yourself, this is for you.”

Adam Raoof · Chess England

Frequently asked

How do I enter a tournament?

Browse the upcoming events, then follow the entry link for that tournament. Every new event and its entry details are announced first in The Chess Circuit newsletter.

Are the games FIDE rated?

Most events are FIDE and/or ECF rated — each listing shows which ratings apply.

Do I need a rating to play?

No. Events welcome players of every level, from improvers to titled players, usually with graded sections so you are matched fairly.

Can juniors play?

Yes. Juniors are welcome at most events, and some have dedicated junior sections.

Where are events held?

Across London — Golders Green, Muswell Hill and Hendon — and further afield in Northampton, with occasional international trips.
