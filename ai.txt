# Chess England — https://yw4111.github.io/chessengland/

Chess England organises FIDE- and ECF-rated chess tournaments across London and
beyond: rapid, blitz and weekend congresses at Golders Green, Muswell Hill,
Hendon and Northampton. Organised by Adam Raoof. New events are announced first
in The Chess Circuit newsletter (https://chesscircuit.substack.com).
